Overview

Arise: System Awakening ("the App", "we", "our") is a gamified habit tracker developed as an independent personal project. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it uses that information, and your rights regarding your data.

✅ Short version: All your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information to any server or third party.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

03

How Data Is Stored

All data is stored exclusively on your own device using AsyncStorage, a standard local key-value storage mechanism provided by React Native. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device.
  • We have no access to your data at any time.
  • No internet connection is required to use the App.
  • Your data is not backed up to any cloud service by us.

ℹ️ Note: Android's OS-level backup (if enabled by you in your device settings) may back up local app storage to your personal Google account. This is controlled entirely by you and your device settings — not by this App.

Completed daily missions are automatically pruned at midnight — only entries matching the current calendar date are retained in storage.

04

How Data Is Used

The data stored locally on your device is used solely to provide the App's core functionality:

  • Display your player name and profile on the Dashboard screen.
  • Track your XP, level, and Hunter Rank progression.
  • Record which daily missions you have completed.
  • Maintain your S.P.E.C.I.A.L. stat point totals.
  • Automatically reset mission progress at the start of each new day.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond the in-app experience described above.

05

Data Sharing

✅ We do not share, sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your personal data to any third party, under any circumstances.

Since all data remains locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server, there is no data that could be shared. There are no:

  • Advertising networks or data brokers receiving your data.
  • Analytics platforms tracking your usage.
  • Business partners or affiliates with access to your data.
  • Government disclosures (as we hold no data to disclose).
06

Third-Party Services

The App is built on open-source frameworks and SDKs. The following are used to power the App's functionality. None of these services receive your personal data:

  • Expo SDK — The application framework. Used for building and running the App. Does not collect personal data from this App.
  • React Native AsyncStorage — Local storage library. All data remains on-device.
  • expo-image — Image rendering for mission media. Local assets only.

The App provides optional external links within mission descriptions (e.g., YouTube tutorials, Project Gutenberg). These links open in your device's browser. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external websites.

⚠️ When you tap an external link, you leave this App and are subject to the privacy policy of the destination website.

07

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Since no data is ever transmitted from the device to any server, the App does not knowingly collect, use, or share children's information remotely. All data entered (name, age) is stored locally on the child's or guardian's device and is never shared.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about information stored on your device, you may delete the App at any time, which will erase all locally stored data.

08

Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and direct control over it:

  • Access: All data is visible within the App's Dashboard and Missions screens.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the App from your device permanently deletes all associated data from AsyncStorage.
  • Correction: Profile data (name, age) can be corrected by clearing the App's data in your device settings and restarting the onboarding flow.
  • Portability: As data is not held by us, we cannot export it on your behalf. The data exists only on your device.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or other regions with data protection laws: since we do not process or store your data on any server, the obligations of GDPR and equivalent regulations are not applicable to us as a data controller. You are the sole controller of your data.
